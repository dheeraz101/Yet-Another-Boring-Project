# YABP Brand and Curation Terms of Use

Welcome to the Yet Another Boring Project (YABP) initiative. YABP is a community registry of minimalist, productive, and open-source projects.

By listing your project in the directory, using the YABP badge, or utilizing YABP branding assets, you agree to these terms.

---

## 1. Brand Guidelines and Logo Usage

The YABP logo and name signify a dedication to clean, distraction-free utility.

*   **Logo Badge Requirement**: Projects listed in the registry must display the YABP logo badge in their repository readme as outlined in the contribution documentation.
*   **Visual Integrity**: You can scale the logo size to match your readme format, but you must not alter the colors or core design elements.
*   **Accurate Representation**: Do not claim that the YABP core maintainers built or endorse your project beyond its status as a registered community listing.

## 2. Directory Curation Policies

*   **Review and Acceptance**: Curation is managed manually. We reserve the right to decline any submission pull request that does not meet our utility and minimalist design standards.
*   **Listing Maintenance**: We reserve the right to remove a registry listing at any time without notice if the project:
    *   Becomes broken, abandoned, or offline.
    *   Violates YABP aesthetic design principles.
    *   Introduces trackers, advertisements, or telemetry.
    *   Deletes its open-source license.
    *   Fails to include the YABP logo badge in its readme.

## 3. Disclaimers

*   **Registry Provision**: This showcase is provided on an "as-is" basis. We make no commitments regarding website uptime or reliability.
*   **Third-Party Projects**: Listed items link to external repositories. We do not audit or guarantee the code quality, security, or privacy policies of these external tools. Use third-party listings at your own risk.
*   **Limitation of Liability**: YABP maintainers and contributors are not liable for damages or losses arising from the use of listed projects or the removal of listings from the register.

## 4. Conduct Standards

We expect all participating developers to:
*   Maintain collaborative communication on issues and PRs.
*   Respect user privacy and licensing attributions.
*   Avoid adding malicious, scam, or tracking systems to member tools.
