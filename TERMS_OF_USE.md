# YABP Brand & Community Terms of Use

Welcome to the **Yet Another Boring Project (YABP)** initiative. YABP is a curated, community-driven ecosystem of open-source projects built on minimal design, reliability, and utility.

By submitting your project to the YABP directory, using the YABP branding, or displaying the YABP badge, you agree to comply with these terms.

---

## 1. YABP Brand & Logo Usage

The YABP logo (`logo.png`) and name represent a commitment to clean design and productivity.

- **Requirement to Display Badge:** Any project listed in the YABP directory must include the YABP logo badge in its README file as described in the [CONTRIBUTING.md](file:///d:/Github_Projects/Yet-Another-Boring-Project/CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Modification Restrictions:** You may scale the logo to fit your README, but you must not alter the logo's colors, shapes, or design features without permission.
- **Fair Representation:** You must not claim that the YABP core team has developed, sponsored, or endorsed your project beyond its status as a curated community listing.

## 2. Directory Listing Policy

- **Curated Selection:** Listing in the YABP directory is a privilege, not a right. Submissions are reviewed manually. We reserve the right to reject any pull request for any reason, including failure to meet our design and quality standards.
- **Removal of Listings:** We reserve the right to remove any project listing from `projects.json` at any time, without prior notice, if:
  - The project becomes inactive, broken, or abandoned.
  - The project changes its design or features in a way that violates YABP minimalist principles.
  - The project introduces malicious code, ads, telemetry, or dark patterns.
  - The project repo is deleted, or fails to maintain its open-source license.
  - The project fails to display the YABP logo badge in its README.

As a community-driven brand, we expect all developers listing projects to:
